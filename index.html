WHAT WE DO

Leak detection for water, gas & drainage

Every job starts the same way: find the leak precisely, before any digging or demolition starts.

01

Water Leak Detection

Rising bills, damp patches, or a spinning water meter with everything switched off — we trace supply-pipe and slab leaks using acoustic correlation and thermal imaging, indoors and out.

02

Gas Leak Tracing

Tracer-gas surveys locate escapes beneath patios, driveways and solid floors that a standard gas safety check can't reach, with readings logged on site.

03

Drainage & Damp Investigation

CCTV drain surveys, moisture mapping and nitrate/chlorine testing to confirm whether a stain is a live leak, an old one, or something else entirely.

HOW WE WORK

Four steps, zero guesswork

The same equipment utility companies use — deployed on your property, with a written report at the end.

1. Listen

Acoustic correlators and ground microphones narrow the search area along the pipe run.

2. Image

Thermal cameras map temperature anomalies under floors, ceilings and render to confirm the hotspot.

3. Confirm

Tracer gas and moisture/nitrate testing rule out false positives before we mark a dig point.

4. Report

You get a marked-up plan and photo evidence — for your own records or to hand to a plumber or insurer.
